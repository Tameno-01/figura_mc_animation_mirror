# Digitigrade leg rig requirements

In this guide, the sections of the digitigrade leg will be called upper leg, lower leg, lower leg 2 and foot. The anatomically correct way to refer to them is Thigh, Calf, Foot and Toes, but the former naming convention is less confusing.

Unless you're using a [vanilla-styled torso](../torso/vanilla_rig_requirements.md), the leg must be directly or indirectly parented to the torso.

The joints can be bent, but only around the X axis.

The foot is optional, but if it's not present, there must be an empty group placed where the foot would be. This is to tell Animation Mirror where the lower leg 2 ends.

If the foot is present, it must be oriented with the toes facing forward (towards negative Z in blockbench).
